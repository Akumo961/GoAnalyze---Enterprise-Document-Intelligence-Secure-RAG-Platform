# GoAnalyze Government — Product Boundary and Commercial Model

## Product

GoAnalyze Government is a proposed secure environmental document-intelligence and decision-support platform. The commercial product should be positioned around evidence traceability, controlled retrieval, analyst workflows, auditability, and deployment flexibility — not around autonomous legal decisions.

## Product layers

- **Core platform:** identity, authorization, tenant isolation, document lifecycle, search, audit, workflow, observability and APIs.
- **Government configuration:** roles, classifications, retention policies, workflow states, deployment policies and customer-approved controls.
- **Environmental integrations:** customer systems, OCR providers, storage, identity, notification and records-management integrations.
- **Customer data:** applications, documents, evidence and operational records owned/controlled by the customer.
- **Regulatory knowledge:** versioned customer-approved sources with provenance and effective dates. Demo data must never be presented as law.
- **Enterprise deployment:** government-hosted, private cloud, on-premises or isolated environments subject to infrastructure validation.

## Analyst workflow target

Ingestion → extraction/OCR → classification → completeness → retrieval → evidence/citations → regulatory mapping → risk/priority → analyst queue → human decision → audit → export.

Every AI result should distinguish evidence, inference, model-generated summarization and uncertainty. Human analysts remain accountable for decisions.

## Commercial packaging — proposed strategy, not market fact

### Tier 1 — Pilot

Synthetic or controlled customer data; limited workflows; measurable baseline; security and architecture validation; success criteria defined before the pilot.

### Tier 2 — Department deployment

Production workflow integration, customer-approved regulatory knowledge, identity integration, operational support, training, monitoring and reporting.

### Tier 3 — Enterprise / multi-ministry program

Multi-environment deployment, integrations, governance, security operations, disaster recovery, expanded workflows, service management and long-term support.

Pricing should be determined through a procurement process and validated scope. This repository does not assert any specific price or contract value.

## Path to a multi-million-dollar program

A program in the multi-million-dollar range would need to be justified by scope and measurable outcomes: software licensing, implementation, integration, infrastructure, security engineering, migration, training, operations, support, governance and expansion across a meaningful organizational footprint. The software repository alone does not establish such value.

A potential ~$9M program would require, at minimum, a successful pilot, quantified workflow improvement using real customer baselines, validated security/privacy architecture, production reliability evidence, authoritative regulatory-data governance, integrations, service-level commitments, staffing/operations model, multi-year roadmap and a procurement mechanism capable of funding that scope.
