# Procurement Readiness

## What is demonstrated

The repository contains a serious application foundation: FastAPI services, PostgreSQL persistence, real Keycloak JWT verification evidence, RBAC/ABAC controls, tenant-scoped data access, hash-chained audit logging, search APIs, object-storage integration, rate limiting, OpenTelemetry integration, infrastructure definitions, tests and CI configuration.

## What is not demonstrated

There is no government customer deployment, government approval, security accreditation, independent penetration-test report, privacy assessment, production-scale OpenSearch/MinIO validation, authoritative Québec regulatory corpus, or proven enterprise analyst workflow in this repository.

## Buyer evaluation sequence

1. Technical discovery and architecture review.
2. Security and privacy assessment.
3. Controlled pilot using synthetic or approved test data.
4. Integration and identity validation.
5. RAG/citation accuracy benchmark.
6. Human-workflow usability evaluation.
7. Reliability, DR and performance validation.
8. Operational/support model validation.
9. Procurement, legal and contractual review.
10. Production acceptance against measurable criteria.

## Commercial contract structure

A credible contract could combine platform licensing/subscription, implementation, integrations, deployment/infrastructure services, security/privacy engineering, training, support and optional expansion. Exact commercial terms must be determined by the customer and procurement process.
