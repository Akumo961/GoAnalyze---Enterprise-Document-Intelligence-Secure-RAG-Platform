# Government Procurement Readiness

## Purpose

This document positions GoAnalyze as software that could be evaluated in a Québec public-sector procurement process. It is not a claim of eligibility, qualification, certification, prequalification, or contract award.

## Product boundary

**Core platform**
- secure API and web console
- document lifecycle and evidence lineage
- tenant-aware authorization
- search and retrieval services
- AI decision-support controls
- audit and observability
- deployment artifacts

**Government configuration**
- workflow profiles
- role and attribute policies
- retention policies
- review queues and SLAs
- approved AI providers
- approved regulatory knowledge packs

**Environment integrations**
- identity provider
- object storage
- databases/search infrastructure
- OCR/document-analysis services
- enterprise notification and records-management systems

**Customer data**
- applications, documents, metadata, evidence, user actions and audit records

**Customer regulatory knowledge**
- authoritative sources loaded, governed, versioned, approved and retired by authorized customer processes

## Commercial packaging (proposed strategy, not market pricing)

### Tier 1 — Pilot

Limited users, synthetic or controlled data, one or two workflows, measurable baseline metrics, security validation, and a defined pilot exit report.

### Tier 2 — Department deployment

Production deployment for a defined organization, enterprise identity integration, governed knowledge packs, operational monitoring, training, support and integration services.

### Tier 3 — Enterprise / multi-ministry

Multiple tenants or ministries, isolated environments where required, high-availability architecture, enterprise integrations, regulatory knowledge governance, security operations, disaster recovery, service management and multi-year support.

A multi-million-dollar program would need to be justified by the contracted scope and measurable delivery outcomes, not by repository size or feature count.

## Buyer evidence expected before a major award

- demonstrated pilot on representative synthetic and then approved controlled records
- repeatable security testing and remediation evidence
- independent penetration testing where required
- privacy and legal assessment by the customer
- identity and access integration evidence
- data residency and deployment architecture evidence
- disaster recovery and continuity exercises
- operational monitoring and support model
- measurable workflow baseline and pilot results
- transparent limitations and human-accountability controls
- documented software supply-chain and vulnerability-management process

## Québec-specific readiness boundary

The product is designed to be French-ready and bilingual-ready, but legal or regulatory translations must be sourced and approved through an authoritative process. No claim of compliance with Québec privacy, records-management, accessibility, procurement, security or environmental law is made by this repository alone.

## Path to a potential multi-million-dollar program

A program of that scale could be commercially plausible only if procurement scope includes multiple value streams such as platform licensing, implementation, integrations, infrastructure enablement, security engineering, data migration, regulatory knowledge onboarding, training, support, service management and expansion. The actual contract value would depend on the buyer's requirements, procurement mechanism, competition, term, volumes, service levels and negotiated scope.
